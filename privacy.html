Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Short version: the tool collects nothing about anybody. It runs on one private computer, and the only data it holds is the owner’s own access tokens and the owner’s own video files. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the social platforms the owner chose to post to.

  1. Who is responsible. The owner and operator of this software, reachable at frugalfi.de@gmail.com.
  2. What data is processed. OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner’s own social accounts, kept in a file on the owner’s own computer, used only to upload videos to those accounts. The owner’s own video files, titles, descriptions and tags, kept on the owner’s own computer. The scheduled time of each post, kept on the owner’s own computer.
  3. What is not processed. No data about any other person. No visitor data, analytics, cookies or tracking of any kind — this page is a static document. No content belonging to anyone other than the owner.
  4. Who receives data. Only the social platform the owner is posting to, and only what is needed for that post: the video file, the caption and the scheduling information. This happens over the platform’s official API. No data is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.
  5. Storage and deletion. Tokens and videos stay on the owner’s computer until deleted there. Disconnecting a platform inside the tool deletes that platform’s token immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform’s own security settings.
  6. Rights. Since the tool processes no third-party personal data, there are no third-party data subjects. The owner may exercise all rights over their own data directly, including deletion, by removing the tool’s data folder.
  7. Contact. frugalfi.de@gmail.com

FrugalFi Publisher

A private tool, run on one personal computer.

FrugalFi Publisher is software its owner runs on their own computer to publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts — YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest and Threads — through each platform’s official API. It is not a service, there is no sign-up, and nobody else can use it.

The two documents required by the platforms it connects to:

  • Terms of Service
  • Privacy Policy
